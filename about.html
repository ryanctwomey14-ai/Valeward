About Valeward

Valeward was established to apply institutional underwriting and reporting standards to a small portfolio of workforce apartment communities, on behalf of a limited number of private investors.

Origins

The first apartment community underwritten by either principal closed in 2007. Within eighteen months, well-capitalised operators were losing assets that remained fully occupied and cash-generative, on account of how those assets had been financed rather than how they performed.

That experience produced a particular set of priorities. We will pay a somewhat higher price for a better basis. We give limited weight to rent growth assumptions. We are reluctant to reduce reserves. And we will not accept a loan structure capable of removing a performing asset from the partnership.

Valeward was founded in 2016 to pursue a single strategy, in one asset class, across a small number of markets that can be reached within a day. We have declined capital that would have required the firm to acquire faster than it can underwrite.
