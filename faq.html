Money and returns

Offerings typically target a 7–10% annualized cash distribution, a 1.8–2.2x equity multiple over a five to seven year hold, and a 15–20% blended annual rate of return. These are objectives derived from underwriting rather than undertakings. Realized results have ranged from 1.4x to 2.3x and may be materially lower, including a total loss of capital.

Quarterly, by ACH, on the fifteenth of the month following each quarter close. New investments generally commence distributions after the first full quarter of ownership. Where a distribution is to be reduced or suspended, written notice setting out the reason is issued before the payment date.

Time, tax and exit

The target hold period is five to seven years. There is no secondary market, no redemption right and no facility for early withdrawal. Where a disposition or refinancing returns capital earlier, that is an incidental outcome rather than a basis on which to subscribe. We decline subscriptions from investors who indicate they may require the capital before realization.
