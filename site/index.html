Why private real estate
warrants a dedicated
allocation.

Public market exposure is rarely as diversified as it appears

Retirement accounts, deferred compensation and brokerage holdings tend to reprice together. A portfolio diversified only within listed securities remains concentrated in a single factor at precisely the point at which diversification is required.

Taxation is the principal constraint on compounding

Employment and pass‑through income is taxed at the highest marginal rates, and conventional securities provide no offset against it. Real estate remains among the few asset classes in which depreciation is passed through directly to the beneficial owner.

Direct ownership carries an operating obligation

Acquiring property individually delivers the economics of real estate together with the responsibilities of an operating business. A limited partnership interest separates the two, retaining the asset exposure without the management burden.

Four principles governing every acquisition

Full investment approach

Workforce housing

We acquire 1990s–2000s vintage communities renting 25–35% below competing new construction. Rent-to-income ratios at this basis have historically supported occupancy through periods of economic contraction.

Fixed-rate financing

Each asset is financed with fixed‑rate agency or life company debt carrying no less than two years of term beyond the business plan. Valeward does not employ floating‑rate bridge debt.

Operational underwriting

Payroll, turnover, delinquency and insurance are modelled at the asset level before any exit assumption is applied. In Class B communities, value is created through operations rather than through the capital markets.

Verifiable alignment

Principals commit no less than 5% of each equity raise in cash on terms identical to those of limited partners. The general partner’s promote is subordinated to an 8% preferred return.

How returns are generated

A multifamily investment generates return through four independent channels. Their relative contribution varies by asset, by business plan and by point in the market cycle; together they produce the blended return targeted for each offering.

01 — Income

Quarterly distributions

Distributions are targeted at a 7–10% annualized rate on invested capital, remitted by ACH on the fifteenth of the month following each quarter close.

7–10%Target annual cash‑on‑cash

02 — Tax

Depreciation

A cost segregation study is commissioned on each acquisition. Investors typically receive a Schedule K‑1 reflecting a first-year passive loss notwithstanding distributions received in the same period.

Yr 1Accelerated depreciation loss

03 — Growth

Amortization and appreciation

Loan amortization and increases in net operating income both accrue to equity. Commercial valuation derives from income, so operational gains capitalize directly into asset value.

1.8–2.2xTarget equity multiple, 5–7 yr hold

04 — Structure

Passive limited partnership

Valeward assumes all operational, financing and reporting responsibility. Limited partners receive quarterly reporting, distributions and an annual Schedule K‑1.

The four channels combined

Distributions, depreciation, amortization and appreciation do not arrive separately. Underwritten together across a five to seven year hold, they produce the blended annual return targeted for each offering — the figure against which we ask to be measured.
