Valeward  /  Approach

Investment approach

Valeward acquires established apartment communities in growing secondary markets, finances them conservatively and improves them through operations. The approach is deliberately unexceptional; consistency of process is the source of the return.

Current acquisition criteria

A documented mandate is a discipline mechanism. Its purpose is to prevent a capable team from reasoning its way into an unsuitable acquisition in a competitive market.

150–400 Units per community

1985–2005 vintage, Class B garden or mid-rise.

1–2% Population growth, YoY

Sun Belt submarkets compounding ahead of the national average year on year.

20–35% Rent discount

Below competing new construction within a three-mile radius.

3.5x Income to rent

Minimum median household income measured against the in-place rent.

Identifiable Cause of underperformance

Deferred maintenance, inadequate management or below-market leases.

Declining Forward supply

New supply as a share of stock falls after the current delivery wave.
